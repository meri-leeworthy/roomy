## Proposals (ranked)

### 1. The search backfill opens every space DB once a day to index nothing

- **Evidence:** production `scope="search-backfill"`, 24 h — **1,445**
  `progress` lines, **1,445 distinct `spaceDid`**, and **1,437 of them carry
  `rows: 0, indexed: 0`** (99.4 %); the eight with work are the only ones that
  did anything. `roomy_search_backfilled` 590 and `roomy_search_indexer_queue`
  0 for the whole window. Mechanism on `origin/next`:
  `packages/appserver/src/search/backfill.ts:578-589` (`nextCursorSpace`) picks
  the space with the oldest `search_backfill_cursor.updated_at` out of the
  `search_backfill_space` candidate list; `:225` hands it to `sweepOneSpace`,
  which **opens the space DB** (`:237`) and runs `SWEEP_SQL` (`:41-50`) before
  finding nothing, then re-stamps the cursor (`:273-280`) so the space rotates
  to the back of the queue. The candidate list has no removal path and no
  "changed since the last sweep" bit, so the walk repeats forever, one space a
  minute.
- **Why now:** the walk is not free. Every visit is a space-DB open in a process
  whose cache holds 800 and which **evicted 1,062 entries in 24 h** — an open
  that finds nothing still displaces whatever request traffic was resident. The
  visit is also 100 % of the `search-backfill` scope's log volume and 3.1 % of
  everything the appserver logged in 24 h, on the stream that is the primary
  signal for the next stall.
- **Scope sketch:** give `search_backfill_space` a `dirty` flag set by the
  existing `entity_space` insert trigger (the same trigger that registers the
  space — `schema-global.sql:183-187`) and cleared when a sweep finds no rows,
  so a caught-up space is not opened at all; keep the round-robin for dirty
  spaces; demote the empty-visit `progress` line to debug and keep the
  indexed/failed one. Nothing changes about what gets indexed.
- **Acceptance:** a sweep over a candidate list with no dirty space opens **no**
  space DB (a counting fake `openSpaceDb`, in the existing
  `src/e2e/search.test.ts` seam); a space whose materialisation inserts an
  entity is picked up on the next cycle; 24 h after deploy, a space with no new
  entities produces zero `search-backfill` lines.
- **Suggested priority:** P3 — no user-visible failure, but it is a standing
  daily cost against the cache and the log budget that grows with the number of
  spaces.
- **Not already tracked because:** TASK-400/`#529` is the **boot** sweep (one
  pass over every space DB at startup); CF1 is the query cache's hit rate; CF22
  is the connection cache's byte budget. None of them is the continuous
  once-a-minute walk, and no open PR touches `search/backfill.ts`.

**One proposal this tick.** The rest of the day is queue state — a queue that
merged nothing, closed nothing, and now has 34 clean PRs behind a single
reviewer — not new work. I would rather say that than pad the list.
