### 1. A 429 took the bridge offline for 24 minutes; neither the appserver nor its telemetry can see who sent it, and the appserver's own limiter never fires

- Evidence: the bridge logged **396** `429` lines between
  **2026-11-10T14:27:41Z and 14:51:17Z** (first `capacity: getSpaceMembership
  failed … (429)` across many spaces, then `send-retry` and `live-roomy`:
  `Rate limited by server (429)`), against at most 3 in any ten-minute bucket
  over the other six days.
  In the same hour the bridge logged **168** `live-roomy` connection errors
  (baseline 1–6/hour), 22 `send failed; queued for re-offer`, 94 `re-offer
  failed; will retry` and 34 `capacity: … keeping previous decision`. The
  bridge was still reconnecting at 14:51Z.
  The appserver is not the source: `roomy_xrpc_requests_total` records **zero**
  responses with `status="429"` across **302,295** requests since the 2026-11-03
  boot, and no `→ 429` line exists in seven days of logs. A control burst of
  **380** `getMetadata` requests in about a minute from one client was answered
  **200 for every one** (the counter moved by 432, so they reached the process).
  The limiter that should have caught them is configured at
  `packages/appserver/src/xrpc/rateLimit.ts:44-46` (100 requests / 60 s, 120 s
  block) and keyed on the **direct connection IP** unless
  `RATE_LIMIT_TRUST_PROXY=true` (`rateLimit.ts:80-100`), checked before routing
  at `packages/appserver/src/xrpc/router.ts:203-204`.
- Why now: this is a user-visible outage — Roomy→Discord delivery stops while
  the socket cannot reopen, and any Discord message in the window waits for the
  reconnect. The only record of it anywhere is the client's own log line. The
  bridge's amplifier (a failed membership lookup re-queried per gate
  consultation) is fixed by `#590`, but where the 429 came from, and how a
  rejection is counted, is not.
- Scope sketch: make a rejection attributable and make the limit mean "this
  client". Count every rejection in `roomy_xrpc_rate_limited_total{nsid,key_class}`
  and log one warn per block entry naming the key class; key the limiter on the
  real client (honour `X-Forwarded-For` behind the Cloudflare/Railway edge, or
  derive the key from the authenticated DID once auth has run) so a shared
  proxy hop cannot be blocked on another tenant's traffic; surface rejections
  in the last 5 minutes on `/health`. No change to the limit's numbers, and no
  change to the bridge. Whether the edge in front of `api.roomy.space` also
  carries a rate rule is not visible from this account and needs Meri.
- Acceptance: a test that drives `POINTS+1` requests from one key gets N
  rejections, N counter increments, and one log line naming the key class; a
  manual burst against a deployed instance shows the same; the next 429 storm is
  visible in the appserver's own metrics instead of only in the client's log.
- Suggested priority: P2.
- Not already tracked because: `#590` (TASK-455) re-stores the capacity
  decision so a failed lookup stops amplifying, and its scope is the Discord
  member-count error class; TASK-458 (`#595`) is `/metrics` authentication;
  no task mentions the XRPC limiter, its key, or rejection visibility.

requests with no 429 and a 380-request burst accepted. It does **not** prove
which layer sent the 429 — Cloudflare sits in front of `api.roomy.space`
(`server: cloudflare`, `x-railway-edge: cdg1`), and a rate rule there is not
readable from this account. It also cannot show whether the appserver's limiter
is disabled in production or merely keyed on an IP that rotates per connection:
both produce the observed "no rejections", and the difference matters to the
fix. The bridge's 24-minute gap is inferred from its own logs; I did not verify
from the Roomy side which messages were delayed.

The 25 h period is still an inference from eight clusters — 25 h apart, one per
day — and I still cannot name the job that causes it; the only in-process hourly
timer is the maintenance purge, which would fire hourly. The stall is real and
its self-vs-total split is unambiguous; "the storage underneath" is an inference

is Faro's flush latency, not the request), so only p50/p90 are quoted. The
community rooms are unchanged since 2026-10-31 (#bugs), 2026-10-04 (#todos),
