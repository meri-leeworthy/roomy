### 2. The `getSpaces` response cache serves a **2.4%** hit rate because an empty-param signal broadcasts an eviction on every space event

- **Evidence.** Mimir, 6 h window:
  | nsid | hits | misses | evictions | hit rate |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | `space.roomy.space.getActivityFeed` | 257 | 153 | 147 | **62.7%** |
  | `space.roomy.space.getMetadata` | 960 | 1860 | 383 | 34.1% |
  | `space.roomy.room.getMetadata` | 141 | 509 | 147 | 21.7% |
  | `space.roomy.space.getThreads` | 2 | 24 | 22 | 7.7% |
  | **`space.roomy.space.getSpaces`** | **9** | **368** | **327** | **2.4%** |

  `getSpaces` is **749 calls/6 h** (≈3,000/day) and almost every one misses *and* evicts. The cause is in `packages/appserver/src/invalidation/inferSignals.ts`: `invalidateSpace()` at `:102` emits `invalidate("space.roomy.space.getSpaces", {})` at `:105`, and it is fanned into seven handlers — `handleCreateRoom` `:738`, `handleDeleteRoom` `:777`, `handleJoinSpace` `:810`, `handleLeaveSpace` `:820`, `handleAddAdmin` `:833`, and two more — plus `federatedReceiversInvalidation` at `:167`. An **empty** param set is a subset of every cached entry's params, so `evictMatching` (`cache/evictListener.ts:43`) treats it as a broadcast and drops **every caller's** space list. One room created anywhere flushes the space list for everyone.
- **Why now.** This is the identical defect `#330`/TASK-253 fixed for `getActivityFeed` — and its own commit message says so: "the empty-param signal had been flushing every reader's every page". The fix was applied to one nsid and its comment in `cache/index.ts:50` asserts the rule is now uniform ("getThreads is keyed by `spaceId` … getActivityFeed … matched by feed coverage"). `getSpaces` sits in the same `CACHEABLE_NSIDS` set, one line above, and was left broadcasting. The previous run's proposal 3 measured the cache at 35.4% overall and could not say why; this says why for one of the five.
- **Scope sketch.** Decide the intended scope, then encode it. Two readings, and the task should pick one explicitly rather than drift: (a) a `joinSpace`/`leaveSpace`/`addAdmin` genuinely changes *that user's* space list, so the signal should carry `affectedUser`; (b) a `createRoom`/`deleteRoom` changes every member's sidebar and legitimately broadcasts, in which case the cost is inherent and belongs in the cache's documentation rather than being fixed. Measure the split first — `handleJoinSpace`/`handleLeaveSpace` are per-user and are the likely dominant source at this volume.
- **Acceptance.** `getSpaces` hit rate over a 6 h window is materially above 2.4% with `getActivityFeed`'s unchanged, or the task records that the broadcast is deliberate and why the hit rate cannot improve.
- **Suggested priority:** **P2** — 368 misses and 327 evictions per 6 h on a route every client calls on load, with a fix shape already proven on the sibling nsid.
