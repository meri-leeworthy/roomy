## Proposals (ranked)

### 1. A bridged room rename that fails is dropped forever: 93 rooms were left with a stale name in one 24 h window, and nothing re-syncs them

- **Evidence.** 24 h of `discord-bridge`: **107 `Failed to update Roomy channel <id> …` lines at `level=error`, across 93 distinct room ids, every one of them `XRPC space.roomy.space.sendEvents timed out after 20000ms`**, clustered in two hours (87 at 10-06T15Z, 20 at 16Z) and all in `did:plc:qzie4v7qwnv3mzaflihrf56f`. The mechanism is a source read on `eaa12205`, `packages/discord-bridge/src/services/room-sync.ts`: `handleRoomUpdate` calls `roomy.sendEvent` directly inside a `try` whose `catch` only logs (`:283-284`, log at `:290`), and `ensureRoomyChannel` (`:63-64`) and `handleRoomDelete` (`:325-326`) have the identical shape. **This is the only such path left.** `message-ingestion.ts:22` and `message-edit-delete.ts:20` import `sendEventOrQueue` from `services/send-retry.ts`, which writes a durable `failed_sends` row (`UNIQUE (space_did, op, discord_id)`, retried by a 60 s sweep registered at `index.ts:311`) — that is why the message traffic in the same two hours recovered and these did not. A rename has no queue entry, no retry, and **no later reconcile**: `handleRoomUpdate` fires only on a Discord `CHANNEL_UPDATE`/`THREAD_UPDATE` (`index.ts:389`), backfill creates rooms but does not re-apply names to ones already mapped, and `getTargetSpacesForChannel` is used elsewhere only for message routing. So a room renamed while the appserver was slow keeps its old name until somebody renames it in Discord again, and the user cannot tell.
- **Why now.** It is a silent, permanent divergence caused by the write path's tail (the same 20 s timeout as the `sendEvents` p50 in the ref state above), and it is the half of the bridge's durability story that did not get the fix the other half did — so the failure looks like a one-off log line rather than the data loss it is.
- **Scope sketch.** Route the three non-message `roomy.sendEvent` call sites in `room-sync.ts` through `sendEventOrQueue` with an op that fits the existing `SendOp` union (`room_update` / `room_delete`), so a failed rename is queued and re-offered like a message. If extending the enum is judged too broad, the narrower alternative is a reconcile: on `ready`, or on the same 5-minute timer that already runs `checkAllBridges`, compare each mapped room's name against its Discord channel and re-issue the update when they differ. Which of the two is right is a brief decision, not a worker's.
- **Acceptance.** A `CHANNEL_UPDATE` whose `sendEvents` is forced to fail leaves a `failed_sends` row and re-offers it, or — under the reconcile option — a room deliberately renamed in Roomy is restored to its Discord name within one sweep; and the `Failed to update Roomy …` line is no longer the only record of a dropped rename.
- **Suggested priority:** **P2** — user-visible and permanent, but cosmetic (a stale room name) and the trigger is the appserver being slow rather than a defect in the rename itself.
- **Not already tracked because.** TASK-273/`#367` (`e7804014`) built the durable queue and applies it to message traffic; nothing in the queue mentions rooms, channel names, or `room-sync`. TASK-294/`#387` is the bridge's *reply* rendering, a different file.

### 3. `backlogStuck` is latched `true` again — this time on a one-row backlog — and it has held for 1,271 sweep cycles

- **Why now.** Three consecutive runs (10-01, 10-02, 10-05) used `backlogStuck` to judge the sweeper, and the 10-05 run leaned on it in the ref-state section. A health field that reads `true` on a healthy one-row backlog cannot report a real stall, and the cost of leaving it is that the next genuine stall looks exactly like this.
- **Scope sketch.** Narrow the latch, not the vocabulary: `setStall` should fire only when the backlog is non-empty, nothing was selected, **and** `selectableRows === 0` is not fully explained by parked rows — i.e. `lastCycle.pendingRows > lastCycle.parkedRows` (there is a row that is neither selectable nor parked, which is the actual stuck shape), or the oldest pending row is older than `STALL_AGE_MS` **and** the parked population is not waiting out a scheduled attempt. Keep `lastStallCause` and the transition counter as they are; they already tell the truth.
- **Acceptance.** On a backlog whose only rows are inside their backoff windows, `backlogStuck` reads `false` and `roomy_embed_backlog_stuck` is 0; a backlog that genuinely stops draining still reads `true`. A unit test on the classifier for both shapes.
- **Suggested priority:** **P3** — no user impact, but it is the third occurrence of a signal the loop has repeatedly used to reason about the sweeper.
- **Not already tracked because.** The 10-03 run explicitly decided **not** to propose a fix after the backlog drained ("Recorded, not proposed again") and the 10-05 run cited `backlogStuck false` as evidence of health. Neither run saw it latched on a one-row backlog.
